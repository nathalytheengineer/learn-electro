About “exact” matches: A text description cannot uniquely identify every schematic mark. A crossed contact can mean a physical normally closed contact or a PLC XIO instruction depending on the drawing. This tool shows representative teaching symbols and alternatives. Confirm the drawing legend, device tag, and applicable standard before treating a match as exact. It is not a wiring or safety design tool.
Descriptions are sent to OpenAI through a server-side function for AI search. The API key stays on the server; symbol data and drawings remain catalog-backed.
